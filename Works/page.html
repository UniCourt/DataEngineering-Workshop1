What is the difference between A4, Letter, and Legal page sizes?

A4 (210×297 mm) is the international standard used in most countries. US Letter (8.5×11 inches / 216×279 mm) is standard in the United States and Canada. US Legal (8.5×14 inches / 216×356 mm) is a taller format used for legal documents. We provide sample PDFs in all three sizes so you can test page-size detection and print layout handling.
